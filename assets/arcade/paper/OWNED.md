# Notebook-paper art — original (owned)

**Source:** `tools/art/gen_paper.py` (Pillow). Full rights, no third-party assets.
Used by the pen-and-paper games (Three in a Row, Word Gallows).

| File | Motif |
|------|-------|
| `paper.png` | tileable cream ruled notebook paper (opaque) |
| `torn.png` | ragged torn-edge strip for the top of the sheet |

The Word Gallows stage sprites (`../gallows/stage0..6.png`, `win.png`) are drawn as
graphite doodles by `tools/art/gen_gallows_notebook.py` to sit on this paper.
