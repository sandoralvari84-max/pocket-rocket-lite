**Source:** `tools/art/gen_putt_art.py` (Python + Pillow). Full rights, no
third-party assets. Small tileable textures used with
`<ImageBackground resizeMode="repeat">`; collision rects are unchanged.

|------|-----|

| `water.png` | Water hazard (ripple arcs) |
| `sand.png` | Bunker (speckled) |
| `wood.png` | Wall / obstacle (plank grain) |
